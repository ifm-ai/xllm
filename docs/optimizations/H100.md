# H100 (sm_90a) report

See [README.md](README.md) for the branch contents, configuration fields and tests.

8 x H100 SXM 80 GB (700 W), FSDP2, per-rank batches, 12-step means. `symm` = `XLLM_FSDP_SYMM=1 NCCL_CTA_POLICY=2`.
xattn is the locally patched 1.1.1 (`docs/optimizations/patches/`, see below); DeepGEMM was a local build
(DeepGEMM on the path, 120 SMs) of the first integration, see the note under the table.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| gekko-0.9B B2 L16384 | 865 ms | 578 | 510-515 | **465-468** | 454 | 69 |
| gekko-4B B1 L8192 | 667 | 502 | 410-417 | **393-405** | 388 | 55 |
| gekko-7.1B B1 L8192 (swift SCA) | 1003 | 770 | 608-613 | **584-586** (`XLLM_FP8_KEEP_X=1 XLLM_GEKKO_SAVE_RMX=1`, bf16 reduce-scatter) | 574-575 | 68 |

DeepGEMM column: measured with the first integration (then `XLLM_FP8_DEEP_GEMM=1`: forward / dgrad GEMMs through DeepGEMM,
weight gradient on cuBLASLt, 120 SMs). The branch carries the H200 integration instead (`XLLM_FP8_DEEPGEMM=1`: the
weight gradient goes through DeepGEMM too with `WGRAD_TILE=x`, all SMs, `XLLM_FP8_DEEPGEMM_SMS` caps them), which was
not re-measured on H100; [H200.md](H200.md) has its numbers.

Speed-ups against the baseline: 1.86x / 1.68x / 1.71x. The bf16 stages (kernel ports, fusions, FSDP / NCCL
configuration) give ~70% / 60% / 55% of the saving, fp8 the rest. On H100 the bf16 step runs at the power cap, the
fp8 step does not (655 W at 1850 MHz).

fp8 on H100 (`xllm/modules/fused_ops/fp8_sm90.py`): blockwise e4m3 (activations 1 x 128, weights 128 x 128
tiles), cuBLASLt; the residual adds run in the GEMM epilogue through an `xllm_extension` op
(`ops.fp8_blockwise_gemm`, `python setup.py build_ext --inplace`); DeepGEMM (opt-in `XLLM_FP8_DEEPGEMM=1`) is bitwise equal to cuBLASLt
on the same operands and was 1.03-1.14x faster at mid shapes here (1.10-1.35x on H200); `XLLM_FP8_AG=1` all-gathers the block's linear
weights as fp8 (2.6-6 GiB less, same speed). Validated by 150-step (7.1B) and 200-step (4B, 0.9B) soaks against bf16
(7.1B mean +2e-4, max 4e-3).

xattn patches (`docs/optimizations/patches/xattn-1.1.1-local-full.patch`, the two partial patches are subsets):
stage-2 hdim-256 varlen backward, GQA pair K/V reuse in the backward, precomputed dO.O, vectorized GQA cast. They
are applied to the installed `xattn` 1.1.1 in the venv; without them the 0.9B / 4B attention backward is 1.1-1.2x
slower.

Open decisions on H100: making `fp8_gemm`, `XLLM_FSDP_SYMM=1` and DeepGEMM defaults; pushing the
xattn patches upstream; installing DeepGEMM as a pinned wheel (H200 built 2.8.2 with gcc-13, see H200.md).
